Teaching philosophy

I believe standards should be high and students learn more by being challenged.

“Those who aim for the moon and fail land on top of the trees. Those who aim for the top of the trees never get off the ground.”

Setting high goals takes students further. They will not always succeed, but they will be better for the effort. Balanced with each student’s abilities, my plan is to challenge them and facilitate their success.

This philosophy aligns with the goals of Career Technical Education, which prepares students for success in both academic and technical fields.

Career Technical Education (CTE) provides students of all ages with the academic and technical skills, knowledge, and training necessary to succeed in future careers and to become lifelong learners.

Background

I teach Web Page Design and Web Application Development at Coalinga High School. My teaching experience spans more than 20 years and includes Computer Applications I and II, Computer Programming, and Introduction to Computer Science with JavaScript.

Before becoming a teacher, I developed intranet websites, applications, and databases, primarily for AT&T. Today, I bring that experience into the classroom and create the teacher tools and interactive student labs featured on this site.
